Where Are You From?

Latin American Edition · Culture-based language learning

Goal

Ask and answer about countries/cities.

Formula

Where + be + subject + from?
I am from Ecuador.
Subject + live(s) + in + city.

Vocabulary

country, city, capital, from, live, mountains, coast, river, Ecuador, Colombia, Peru, Latin America.

Reading

Quito, Bogotá, and Lima

Quito, Bogotá, and Lima are important capital cities in Latin America. Quito is the capital of Ecuador, Bogotá of Colombia, and Lima of Peru. These cities share history, culture, and strong Latin American identity.

Quito is in the Andes Mountains. It has plazas, old churches, and museums. Bogotá is also in the mountains, a large city with libraries and parks. Lima is on the coast of Peru, near the Pacific Ocean, famous for food and ocean views.

Check

A. Complete.

  1. Quito is the capital of .
  2. Bogotá is the capital of .
  3. Lima is the capital of .
  4. Quito is in the Mountains.

B. Answer.

  1. What can people see in Quito?
  2. What is Lima famous for?
