Where Are You From?

Latin American Edition · Culture-based language learning

Learning Goal

I can ask and answer about countries and cities.

Grammar Formula

Where + be + subject + from?
Where + are + you + from?
I + am + from + Ecuador.
Subject + live(s) + in + city.
I live in Quito.

Key Vocabulary
country city capital from live mountains coast river Ecuador Colombia Peru Latin America
Culture Reading

Quito, Bogotá, and Lima

Quito, Bogotá, and Lima are important capital cities in Latin America. Quito is the capital city of Ecuador. Bogotá is the capital city of Colombia. Lima is the capital city of Peru. These three cities are in different countries, but they share history, culture, language, and strong Latin American identity.

Quito is in the Andes Mountains. It has plazas, old churches, museums, and views of volcanoes. The historic center is famous because its streets and buildings show the past of the city. Quito is also a modern city with schools, universities, parks, and families from many places in Ecuador.

Bogotá is also in the mountains. It is a large city with libraries, museums, universities, parks, and busy streets. Many people in Bogotá use buses, bicycles, and public transportation. The city is an important place for education, art, music, and government in Colombia.

Lima is on the coast of Peru, near the Pacific Ocean. It is famous for food, history, and ocean views. Lima connects ancient culture with modern city life. Quito, Bogotá, and Lima show that every country has places, people, traditions, and stories.
