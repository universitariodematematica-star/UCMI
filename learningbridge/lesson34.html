Before Reading

What helps students study well at home or school?


Study Habits and Responsibility

Good study habits help students learn. A study habit is an action that students do often to practice and remember information. Students can read notes, write sentences, review vocabulary, practice pronunciation, complete homework, and ask questions. These habits are simple, but they are very useful.

In many Latin American homes, students study in different places. Some students study at a desk. Others study at a table, in a bedroom, in a library, or in a quiet corner of the house.

A good study space does not need to be perfect. It needs to be organized, calm, and useful for learning. Responsibility is important for study. Students can organize notebooks, prepare materials, follow a schedule, and practice a little every day. Family members can support students, but students also need personal effort and discipline.

In English, students can say: “I study English.” “I read my notes.” “I write sentences.” “I practice every day.”
