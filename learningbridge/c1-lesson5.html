C1 Class: Comparing Dreams and Goals in Life

Welcome to this advanced English session. Today we will explore how to express long-term goals, balance family and professional responsibilities, and use C1-level vocabulary and grammatical structures[cite: 4].

1. Essential Vocabulary

Verb Phrases

  • fulfill a dream: to achieve a lifelong ambition[cite: 4]
  • set a goal: to establish an objective[cite: 4]
  • work towards / pursue a goal: to make efforts to achieve an objective[cite: 4]
  • put [something] off: to postpone something[cite: 4]
  • share responsibilities: to divide duties fairly[cite: 4]

Adjectives

  • ambitious: having a strong desire for success[cite: 4]
  • modest: unassuming or moderate[cite: 4]
  • achievable: possible to accomplish[cite: 4]
  • unachievable: impossible to accomplish[cite: 4]
  • realistic: having a sensible idea of what can be achieved[cite: 4]
  • unrealistic: not based on reality[cite: 4]

2. Advanced Grammar Focus

At the C1 level, we combine different verb tenses to bring dynamism to our life stories and plans[cite: 4]:

  • Present Continuous for medium/long-term projects: "I'm fulfilling my lifelong dream to be an archaeologist..."[cite: 4]
  • Future Continuous for extended plans: "My husband will be working from home for the next three years..."[cite: 4]
  • First Conditional for mutual commitments: "If we have a common goal and work towards it, anything's achievable."[cite: 4]
  • Separable Phrasal Verbs: "My wife put off her studies..."[cite: 4]

3. Useful Phrases for Discussion & Expression

Expressing Ambitions and Priorities

  • "My primary objective at this stage in life is to..."
  • "I'm fully committed to pursuing my career goals, even if it means making sacrifices."
  • "It's a matter of striking the right balance between personal aspirations and family duties."

Negotiating and Sharing Responsibilities

  • "Since you supported my studies last year, now it's my turn to back you up."
  • "We need to divide and conquer when it comes to household chores and childcare."
  • "Let's make sure we don't put off our long-term plans indefinitely."

4. Comprehension and Dialogue Practice (Activate)

Reflection question for class debate:

In what ways can a couple take turns supporting each other's career goals without neglecting the household? Use at least three of the learned verb phrases and one useful phrase from above (e.g., share responsibilities, put off, pursue a goal)[cite: 4].
