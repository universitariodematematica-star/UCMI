Culture Reading

The Legend of Cantuña

The Legend of Cantuña is a famous story from Quito, Ecuador. It is connected with the old city and with the Church of San Francisco. This church is in the historic center of Quito. The legend is part of the oral tradition of the city.

Cantuña is a man in the legend. He is a builder. The story says that he has to finish an important stone construction. In the story, one stone is missing, which changes the ending and shows intelligence and courage.
