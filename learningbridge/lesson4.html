Introducing a Friend

Latin American Edition · Culture-based language learning

Learning Goal

I can use he, she, and this is to introduce another person or cultural topic.

Grammar Formula

This + is + person/topic
This + is + Cantuña.
Subject pronoun + be + complement
He + is + a builder.
She + is + important.
It + is + a legend.

Key Vocabulary
legend church builder stone city story person man he she this is Quito
Grammar in Context

Use this is to introduce a person, place, or topic. This is Cantuña. Use he for a man or boy, she for a woman or girl, and it for a thing or story.

Culture Reading

The Legend of Cantuña

The Legend of Cantuña is a famous story from Quito, Ecuador. It is connected with the old city and with the Church of San Francisco. This church is in the historic center of Quito. The legend is part of the oral tradition of the city. Oral tradition means that people tell a story from one generation to another.

Cantuña is a man in the legend. He is a builder. The story says that he has to finish an important stone construction. The work is difficult, and the time is short. The legend has mystery, night, stones, and a problem that Cantuña must solve.

In the story, one stone is missing. This detail is very important. Because one stone is missing, the work is not complete. The missing stone changes the ending of the legend. Many people remember this part because it shows intelligence and courage.

The Legend of Cantuña is not only an old story. It is a cultural symbol of Quito. It connects a person, a church, a city, and the imagination of the people. Legends like this help communities remember their places, their values, and their history.
