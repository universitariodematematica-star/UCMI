Culture Reading

Inti Raymi

Inti Raymi is an important Andean celebration. The name means “Festival of the Sun.” It is celebrated in June in Ecuador, Peru, and other Andean regions. The celebration is connected with the sun, the earth, the harvest, and community life. In Andean culture, the sun gives light, warmth, and life to the land.

During Inti Raymi, people meet in towns, plazas, and community spaces. There is music, dance, traditional clothing, and food. The colors are bright, and the atmosphere is respectful. Families, dancers, musicians, and visitors can be part of the celebration. Many people give thanks for nature and for the work of the community.

Greetings are also important. People say hello, good morning, welcome, and nice to meet you when visitors arrive. These words are simple, but they show respect. In English, the same greetings help people from different places communicate. A greeting can open a conversation and create a friendly connection.

Inti Raymi is not only a festival. It is a cultural memory. It is a way to remember history, nature, identity, and the relationship between people and the land. It shows that traditions are alive and important.
