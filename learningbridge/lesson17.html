Culture Reading

School Subjects in Latin America

Students in Latin America study many school subjects. These subjects help students understand language, numbers, nature, history, art, sports, and community life. A school day can include English, math, science, history, art, music, physical education, and other local subjects.

Each subject has a purpose. English helps students communicate with people from different countries. Math helps students solve problems and understand numbers in daily life. Science helps students learn about plants, animals, water, health, and the environment. History helps students understand their country and region.

Art and music are also important. In many Latin American schools, students can draw, sing, dance, play instruments, or learn about traditional music and celebrations. Physical education helps students move, play, and build healthy habits.

In English, students can talk about school subjects with simple sentences: “I study English.” “I like science.” “My favorite subject is art.” These sentences help students connect school life with personal interests and future goals.
