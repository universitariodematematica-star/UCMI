My School

Latin American Edition · Culture-based language learning

Learning Goal

I can describe my school and name important school places.

Grammar Formula

This + is + my + place
This is my school.
This is my classroom.
There + is + singular noun
There is a library.
v
There + are + plural noun
There are classrooms.

Key Vocabulary
school clasroom library playground office teacher student community learn place
Grammar in Context

Use This is my... to present a place.
Use there is for one place or object.
Use there are for more than one place or object.

Culture Reading

Friendship and Cooperation

A school is an important place in many Latin American communities. It is not only a building with classrooms. A school can be a place for learning, friendship, family meetings, cultural events, sports, and community projects. Students, teachers, parents, and neighbors can all be connected to school life.

A school usually has classrooms, an office, a playground, bathrooms, and sometimes a library, a computer room, or a garden. In some rural areas, a school can be small, but it can still be very important. In cities, schools can be larger and have many students from different neighborhoods.

Schools also preserve culture. Students can celebrate local festivals, learn about national symbols, prepare presentations, sing songs, or participate in civic events. A school can help students understand their country, their community, and the world.

In English, students can describe school places with simple sentences: “This is my school.” “There is a library.” “There are classrooms.” These sentences help students talk about a familiar place in a clear and useful way.
