Describing People

Latin American Edition - Culture-based language learning

Learning Goal

I can describe people with simple adjectives.

Grammar Formula

Subject + be + a/an + occupation
She is a teacher.
He is a farmer.
He is an artist.
What + be + his/her + job?
What is her job?

Key Vocabulary
teacher doctor farmer seller driver artist worker market school community
Grammar in Context

Use a before most job words: a teacher, a
doctor, a farmer.
Use an before a vowel sound: an artist.
Use he is and she is to talk about
occupations.

Before Reading

What jobs are important in your community?

Culture Reading

Community Workers in Latin America

Every community needs many workers. In Latin America, a community can include teachers, farmers, sellers, doctors, drivers, artists, builders, and many other people. Each job is important because each person helps the community in a different way.

A teacher helps students learn. A farmer grows food for families and markets. A seller works in a store or market and helps people buy what they need. A doctor helps people with health. A driver moves people from one place to another. An artist can share music, painting, dance, or traditional crafts.

In many towns and cities, work is connected with family and community life. A market seller can know many families. A farmer can bring local products to town. A teacher can help children and parents. These jobs are part of daily culture, not only money or business.

In English, students can identify occupations with simple sentences: “She is a teacher.” “He is a farmer.” “He is an artist.” Learning job words helps students talk about people around them with respect.
