Age and Birthdays

Latin American Edition · Culture-based language learning

Learning Goal

I can say my age and talk about birthdays.

Grammar Formula

How old + be + subject?
How old are you?
I am twelve years old.
My birthday + be + in + month.
My birthday is in May.

Key Vocabulary
age birthday family celebration cake music month years old party tradition
Grammar in Context

Use How old are you? to ask about age.
Use I am + number + years old to answer.
Use My birthday is in + month to talk about birthday month.

Before Reading

How do families celebrate birthdays in your community?

Culture Reading

Birthdays and Family Celebrations

Birthdays are important family celebrations in many parts of Latin America. A birthday is a special day to remember a person's life and show love, friendship, and gratitude. Families can celebrate at home, at school, in a park, or in a community space.

A birthday celebration can be simple or big. Some families prepare a cake, music, balloons, food, and games. Friends and relatives can say "Happy birthday" and give good wishes. In some places, people sing traditional birthday songs. In other places, families mix local traditions with modern celebrations.

Birthdays also help students practice age and months in English. A student can say, "I am eleven years old" or "My birthday is in August." These sentences are short, but they are useful for introductions, school forms, and conversations.

A birthday is more than a party. It is a moment of family connection. It shows that people are important to their community. When students talk about birthdays in English, they also talk about family, time, culture, and personal identity.
