Advanced English Module

Mastering the Grammar of Goals and Life Projects

The analysis of advanced linguistic structures allows us not only to express everyday actions, but also long-term plans, reflections on social roles, and complex arguments.
Based on the study units regarding personal and family goals, four fundamental grammatical pillars emerge that enhance fluency in English. Explore each topic below along with its practical utility and contextual examples.

1. Verb Phrases: Infinitives, Gerunds, and Phrasal Verbs

What they are used for:
They are used to connect main actions with other activities, desires, ongoing processes, or changes in direction (using particles), allowing ambitions, goals, or routines to be expressed fluently and precisely.
  • 1. English: She hopes to finish her doctoral thesis by the end of the year.
    Español: Ella espera terminar su tesis doctoral para finales de año.
  • 2. English: He enjoys working on complex mathematical logic frameworks.
    Español: Él disfruta trabajando en marcos complejos de lógica matemática.
  • 3. English: They decided to put off the project until they gathered more data.
    Español: Ellos decidieron posponer el proyecto hasta reunir más datos.

2. Logical Connectors and Conditional Sentences

What they are used for:
They are used to structure causal reasoning and hypothetical thinking, which is essential for justifying decisions, explaining why things happen, or anticipating outcomes under certain conditions.
  • 1. English: The research advanced quickly because the team shared a clear vision.
    Español: La investigación avanzó rápidamente porque el equipo compartía una visión clara.
  • 2. English: If we apply the general algorithm, the solution will become evident.
    Español: Si aplicamos el algoritmo general, la solución se hará evidente.
  • 3. English: She stayed home to take care of the children while her husband worked.
    Español: Ella se quedó en casa para cuidar de los niños mientras su esposo trabajaba.

3. The Passive Voice with Modals in Social Debates

What they are used for:
They are used to shift the focus of attention to the object of the action, the norm, or the concept being analyzed rather than focusing on who performs it, providing a more formal, objective tone suitable for debates or theoretical arguments.
  • 1. English: Such theoretical frameworks should be analyzed with rigorous precision.
    Español: Dichos marcos teóricos deben ser analizados con rigurosa precisión.
  • 2. English: Can educational resources be accessed by all students in the community?
    Español: ¿Pueden los recursos educativos ser consultados por todos los estudiantes de la comunidad?
  • 3. English: Household responsibilities must be shared equally by both partners.
    Español: Las responsabilidades del hogar deben ser compartidas equitativamente por ambos miembros de la pareja.

4. Lexical Precision: Participial Adjectives and Suffixation

What they are used for:
They are used to assess the feasibility of projects and precisely define the roles, functions, or characteristics of individuals within a social, academic, or professional context.
  • 1. English: The proposed mathematical proof is both ambitious and achievable.
    Español: La demostración matemática propuesta es tanto ambiciosa como alcanzable.
  • 2. English: He acts as the primary breadwinner while managing his academic schedule.
    Español: Él actúa como el principal sostén económico del hogar mientras gestiona su horario académico.
  • 3. English: Ignoring these variables would be completely unrealistic for the model.
    Español: Ignorar estas variables sería completamente irrealista para el modelo.
