Brothers, Sisters, and Relatives

Latin American Edition · Culture-based language learning

Learning Goal

I can say how many brothers, sisters, and relatives I have.

Grammar Formula

I + have + number + family member
I have one brother.
I have two sisters.
These + are + my + plural noun
These are my cousins.

Key Vocabulary
brother sister cousin uncle aunt relative one two three many extended family
Grammar in Context

Use I have... to talk about family members.
Use one, two, three and other numbers before plural family words.
Use These are my... to introduce more than one person.

Before Reading

What is an extended family? Do relatives often meet in your community?

Culture Reading

Grandparents and Extended Family

In many Latin American families, relatives are an important part of daily life. An extended family can include grandparents, cousins, uncles, aunts, and other close people. These relatives can live in the same house, near the family, in another city, or even in another country.

Grandparents often have a special place in the family. They can tell stories about the past, teach family traditions, cook traditional food, or help children after school. Cousins can be like friends. Uncles and aunts can help with celebrations, advice, and family activities.

Extended families often meet for birthdays, holidays, local festivals, weddings, and community events. In some communities, family members help each other with work, food, transportation, school, and care. This support is part of family life.

In English, students can say how many relatives they have: "I have one brother." "I have two sisters." "These are my cousins." These sentences help students describe their family in a simple and respectful way.

Reading Check

A. Read and answer.

  1. What people can be part of an extended family?
  2. What can grandparents teach?
  3. Why can cousins be important?
  4. When do extended families often meet?
  5. What sentence says you have one brother?

B. Complete: I have brother(s). I have sister(s).

C. Think: Who is one important relative in your life?
